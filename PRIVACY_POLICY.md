# Privacy Policy for Smart S Launcher

**Effective date:** 20 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 20 September 2026

Smart S Launcher (`com.tbzmike.smartslauncher`) is developed and published by **Tebogo Mahlangu**. This policy explains what information the app can access, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

## Privacy summary

Smart S Launcher is designed to process launcher data on the user's device. The developer does not operate a Smart S Launcher server that receives users' contacts, messages, call logs, notification content, location, app-usage history, launcher history, or battery history.

Smart S Launcher:

- does not contain advertising;
- does not contain analytics or behavioral-tracking services;
- does not sell or rent personal information; and
- does not use personal information for advertising or profiling.

Some optional features need Android permissions or special access. Those features remain off or unavailable unless the user enables them or grants the relevant access.

## Information processed on the device

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, Smart S Launcher may process the following information locally:

### Installed apps, shortcuts, and launcher activity

The app reads information about installed applications, activities, shortcuts, work or secondary profiles, and app labels so that it can act as a home-screen launcher, display apps, launch activities, and provide search results. It also stores launcher preferences, favorites, tags, custom app names, search or launch history, and related local configuration.

If Android Usage Access is enabled, the app can read app-usage and device-interaction information to provide local usage history and statistics. Smart S Launcher keeps its local app-usage timeline for up to 365 days.

### Contacts, calls, and SMS

If the user grants the relevant permissions or enables communication indexing, the app can read contacts, call-log entries, and SMS content made available by Android. This is used to provide contact search, caller-name matching, call or message actions, and local communication search. This information is indexed and searched on the device and is not uploaded to the developer.

If call-screening features are enabled, Android may provide call-related information needed for those features. Smart S Launcher does not use this information for advertising or analytics.

### Notifications

If Notification Access is enabled, the app can read notification information, including the originating app, title, message text, time, icons or avatars, conversation identifiers, and action or destination metadata supplied by Android. The app uses this information for notification dots, notification history, search, opening the original destination when possible, and user-invoked actions such as inline replies.

Notification history is stored locally according to the storage option selected by the user. It may remain until the user deletes it, clears the app's data, uninstalls the app, or Android removes it under a selected storage-aware policy.

### Location

If the optional live-location details feature is enabled and location permission is granted, the app can obtain a current or recent device location to show coordinates and accuracy in a local Google Maps history card. Smart S Launcher does not reverse-geocode this location, download map tiles for this feature, or upload the location to the developer. A location shown in launcher history may remain in the app's local data until that history is deleted.

### Battery and device state

If battery monitoring is enabled, the app stores local samples such as battery level, charging state, screen state, temperature, voltage, current, charge counter, and energy information supplied by Android. Battery samples are retained locally for up to 180 days and are used to display battery history, estimates, and widgets.

### Accessibility Service

The optional Accessibility Service is used only to perform the Android lock-screen action for the double-tap-to-lock feature. The service does not inspect accessibility events, collect screen content, or store accessibility data.

## Network connections and third-party services

Smart S Launcher does not upload the locally processed information described above to a developer-operated server.

The app can make the following network-related actions:

- **Update checks and downloads:** When the user checks for an update or uses the in-app updater, the app connects to GitHub and may use GitHub content endpoints or jsDelivr fallbacks to retrieve release information or an APK. These providers receive ordinary network information such as the user's IP address, request time, and app version in the request's user-agent string under their own privacy practices.
- **External searches and links:** When the user chooses a web search, map search, app-store link, website, call, message, or another external action, Smart S Launcher sends the selected query or action to the app or service chosen by the user. That third party processes the information under its own privacy policy.
- **Android services:** Android and the device manufacturer may process permission, location, notification, package, backup, and other system-service activity under their own terms. The developer does not receive that system data merely because Smart S Launcher requests a system feature.

## Storage, backup, and deletion

App data is stored in Smart S Launcher's private application storage. Android may include eligible app data in device or cloud backups depending on the user's device, Android version, account, and backup settings. Such backups are controlled by Android and the user's backup provider, not by the Smart S Launcher developer.

Users can limit processing by disabling individual features, revoking permissions or special access in Android Settings, deleting available history from within the app, clearing Smart S Launcher's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app deletes the app's on-device data, subject to any independent Android backup retained under the user's backup settings.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell, rent, or share users' locally processed personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Information leaves the app only when needed for an action the user requests, an update connection described above, an Android system function or backup, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

## Security

Smart S Launcher relies on Android's application sandbox, runtime permissions, and special-access controls to protect local data. Users should keep their device, Android security updates, and installed apps secure. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Smart S Launcher is a general-purpose launcher and is not designed to create user accounts or collect personal information on developer-operated servers. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through such a server. Parents or guardians should manage the device permissions and external services available to a child.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Smart S Launcher features or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
