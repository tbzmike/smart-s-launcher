Fix launched apps/shortcuts briefly appearing in History and then disappearing after the authoritative History refresh. RECENCY now orders each unique history identity by its latest database row instead of using DISTINCT with an unrelated _id ordering. Successful app launches also refresh the persistent app catalog, and History can reconstruct an app from that catalog when LauncherApps/provider state temporarily hides it (for example during freeze/unfreeze or provider reload). Warm Home history uses the same persistent fallback.
