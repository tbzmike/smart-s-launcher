Fix a remaining constant Vertical List stutter seen in logcat. Smart S was still running two cold icon/package-resource workers in parallel; AssetManager resource loads continued behind the list and triggered a 14 MB background GC. 3.30.128 reduces icon loading to one worker outside scrolling, interrupts/cancels all active icon tasks immediately when scrolling starts, purges cancelled queued work, and never replays it automatically at idle. A second logcat issue was an unclosed SQLite cursor after a cancellation; cancellable History/shortcut reads now close their cursors in finally/try-with-resources instead of leaving cleanup to GC. All 3.30.127 exact per-notification media-artwork separation and stored notification/message destination routing remain unchanged; no destination-link storage code is modified in this release.
