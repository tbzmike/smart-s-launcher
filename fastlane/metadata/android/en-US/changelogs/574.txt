Fixes intermittent empty/stuck History Home. History refreshes requested while a list/card fling is active are now coalesced and replayed after idle instead of being silently dropped. If a running History load is cancelled by scroll-start, one authoritative recovery pass is remembered. A History worker that aborts because scrolling begins marks itself cancelled so its empty partial state cannot clear the adapter. Pausing Home resets stale scroll gates, covering the case where Android removes focus before the idle callback fires. MainActivity's scroll guard now defers History rather than discarding it, and an already-empty Home is allowed to accept its first non-empty recovery snapshot. Preserves 3.30.145 Appearance wiring, 3.30.144 single-tap/message routing, and the earlier performance/Pixel/frozen-app fixes. Animation wiring is also completed for all History layouts: the selected History/app scrolling style now reaches Vertical List, Vertical Cards and 3D Wheel; 3D Wheel uses a separate content animation layer so scroll-style effects do not overwrite its wheel geometry. Newly visible rows/cards animate once after a fling settles, never during active scrolling, preserving the frame-drop fix. The View/window switching style now also animates entry into Vertical List, Vertical Cards and 3D Wheel. List change animations are again guarded against active scrolling.
