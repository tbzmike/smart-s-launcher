History scrolling is now treated as a render-critical section. Smart S Launcher cancels an active HistorySearcher as soon as scrolling starts, clears queued History seed/preview work, refuses to start new History database work during the gesture, and resumes only one remembered refresh after the active renderer is idle. HistorySearcher is also reduced to one authoritative history database read per pass instead of repeated reads and duplicate recovery passes, reuses one shortcut catalog and notification provider, and checks cancellation throughout long loops. Existing notification deduplication, search app/shortcut bottom ordering, Vertical Cards and 3D Wheel fixes remain unchanged.
