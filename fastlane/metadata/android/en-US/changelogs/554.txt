Vertical List background work is reduced further. History refreshes that arrive during scrolling are now dropped instead of queued for idle, removing a remaining post-scroll rebuild path. A process-wide scroll gate pauses battery telemetry/widget refreshes, scheduled app-usage sync, notification ranking reconciliation, notification avatar bitmap/disk work, stale-route auto-repair and media-history refreshes while the launcher is moving. The notification forwarder no longer duplicates every live notification timeline write on the UI thread; NotificationListener remains authoritative, while resume-time catch-up stays on a background thread. Notification-dot animations are also suppressed during active scrolling.
