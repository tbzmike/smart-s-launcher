Add selectable Standard and Pixel modes for the bottom favorites bar. Standard mode keeps the existing unlimited favorites-only behavior unchanged. Pixel mode keeps pinned favorites first, then fills remaining slots with the most frequently launched apps from Smart S history; total Pixel slots are adjustable from 1 to 10 in Settings. Pixel suggestions are computed only on demand when Pixel mode is active—no predictor service, periodic job, listener loop or background worker is started, and Standard mode never runs the Pixel usage query. Pixel drag reordering is disabled so predicted items cannot be accidentally persisted as favorites; normal Add/Remove favorite actions remain available. Built directly from the 3.30.127 baseline so exact notification/media artwork separation and stored notification destination routing are retained.
