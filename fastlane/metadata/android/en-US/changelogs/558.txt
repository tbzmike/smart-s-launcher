Treat IceBox/wrapper shortcuts and the real target app as one logical app across Smart S Launcher. Pixel mode now keeps frozen/disabled apps in the bar instead of dropping them, uses a matching wrapper shortcut as the launch route when available, and deduplicates the wrapper and real app into one slot. New wrapper launches are recorded under the real app identity, while existing split history is merged when launch counts, today's interactions, timestamps and Pixel most-used rankings are read. Android usage time for wrapper shortcuts is shown from the real target package, and shortcut-invocation usage events are attributed to the target app when the saved shortcut mapping is known. The shortcut catalog now persists target_package so the relationship survives freezing and later provider reloads. Standard favorites mode remains the legacy unlimited favorites-only mode and does not run Pixel ranking work. No new predictor service, periodic job or scroll-time worker is added. The 3.30.127 exact notification/media-artwork separation and stored notification/message destination routing are retained.
