Fix the black-screen/freeze when returning HOME in Pixel favorites mode. Logcat showed Smart S skipping 925 frames and AppProvider/ShortcutsProvider completion being delayed about 7.65 seconds because Pixel prediction called LaunchStatsProvider synchronously from the HOME main thread. Pixel mode now renders its already-loaded pinned/frozen-app slots immediately, then performs launch-stat prediction on one low-priority cancellable worker after HOME has drawn. The worker never runs in Standard mode, is cancelled on mode/activity changes, and is suppressed while launcher scrolling is active. Canonical IceBox/real-app usage merging is preserved, but launch-stat alias merging is now linear-time instead of repeatedly scanning providers. All 3.30.127 notification destination and exact per-event media-artwork fixes remain intact.
