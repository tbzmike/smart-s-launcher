Fixes the touch-dispatch regression introduced by direct icon long-press handling. History rows, active search results and their primary icons now bind single-tap launch and long-press menu actions together, so one normal tap launches immediately instead of being swallowed by a long-clickable child. Vertical Card icons also bind both actions directly. Notification/message body taps remain exact-route actions: WhatsApp and other message previews keep their linked notification/conversation destination, with the existing originating-app fallback only when the exact route is genuinely unavailable. Preserves 3.30.143 Pixel frozen-app launching, 3.30.142 notification-route retention, 3.30.141 prediction/metadata, and 3.30.140 performance fixes.
